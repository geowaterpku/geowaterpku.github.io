GeoWater Lab Peking University

    01

    For the community

    Open data for spatially explicit river science

    This page will bring together datasets and related research products developed by the GeoWater Research Lab. It will provide community access to spatial information, Earth observations, model-derived products, and supporting documentation that help characterize global rivers and their changing dynamics.

    What you will find here

    Data collections

    Resources will be added as individual datasets are documented and released.

    01

    River structure

    Global river networks, channel geometry, hydraulic attributes, floodplains, and river connectivity.

    02

    Flow dynamics

    Runoff and streamflow products, hydrological signatures, river pulses, and model-derived estimates.

    03

    Flood–human interactions

    Data describing floodplain development, infrastructure, exposure, and the changing capacity of river–floodplain systems.

    04

    Earth observations

    Satellite-derived measurements of river water levels, widths, discharge, inundation, and related surface-water dynamics.

    05

    Documentation and reuse

    Methods, metadata, version histories, licenses, citations, and links to code needed for transparent and reproducible use.

    Growing collection

    Datasets are being prepared for release.

    Each entry will include a concise description, access information, documentation, and a preferred citation. For questions about a GeoWater Lab dataset, please contact us or explore the studies behind these products on our Publications page.
