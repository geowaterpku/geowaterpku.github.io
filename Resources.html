GeoWater Lab Peking University

    01

    Open research infrastructure

    Datasets for large-scale, river-centric hydrology

    GeoWater Lab develops and contributes open datasets that make river systems more observable, spatially explicit, and reusable for hydrological research. The collection spans global river networks, naturalized discharge, hydrological signatures, dynamic catchment attributes, and geomorphic floodplains.

    01

    GRADES

    River discharge · global river network

    The first naturalized river discharge dataset at 2.94 million river reaches, developed for network-scale and global analyses of river-flow variability.

    02

    MERIT-Basins

    River network · local catchments

    Global vectorized flowlines and local catchments with a 25 km² channelization threshold.

    03

    MERIT-Hydro-Vectors

    River network · variable drainage density

    Global vectorized flowlines with variable drainage density, enabling more flexible representation of river-network structure across landscapes.

    GSHA

    Large-sample hydrology · dynamic catchment attributes

    Large-sample hydrology datasets with annually varying land-cover statistics for river-centric studies of streamflow characteristics, hydrometeorology, and catchment attributes.
