Core Research Needs

Four pillars guiding our approach to water systems research

🛰️

Observation–Model Fusion

Leverage satellite width, elevation, and snow products with hydrologic routing so ungauged rivers can be diagnosed with the same rigor as instrumented basins.

  • SWOT-informed discharge retrievals
  • Process-oriented model benchmarking
🧪

Process Diagnostics

Quantify forcing and parameter uncertainty to reveal where snowmelt runoff and flood routing need redesign.

  • Noah-MP multi-physics experiments
  • Grid-LSTM reconstructions
🏗️

Human–River Feedbacks

Represent levees, reservoir cascades, and urban encroachment so risk metrics respond to real planning decisions.

  • Floodplain growth accounting
  • Reservoir release spectra
🗺️

Reusable Data Infrastructure

Publish global river attributes that shorten the path from science to policy implementation.

  • GSHA river networks
  • SHIFT floodplain data

03

Scientific Challenges

Three critical barriers to understanding global water systems

01
📉

Sparse & Biased Observations

Gauge and SWOT gaps hamper calibration where flood risk is highest, leaving vulnerable basins unmonitored.

02
🌉

Model Uncertainty

Snow, runoff, and routing physics lack diagnostic feedbacks to identify structural weaknesses.

03
⚡

Human Perturbations

Reservoirs and levees reshape flood extremes nonlinearly without standardized assessment metrics.

Implication: Without unified observation-model-human frameworks, global water security strategies will continue to lag behind the forces reshaping rivers.

GeoWater Lab Solutions

From Observation to Actionable Water Intelligence

We close the loop between satellite-era observations, diagnostic modeling, and policy-ready datasets.

🛰️
Remote Sensing

Observation–Model Fusion

SWOT discharge inversions, GSHA river attributes, and RODEO retrieval algorithms provide global reach-level data where gauges are sparse.

  • Bankfull width climatologies (GRL 2020)
  • Vector-based routing for ESMs (Sci Data 2021)
🧪
Process Diagnostics

Hydrologic Truthing

Multi-physics Noah-MP experiments and grid-LSTM reconstructions expose where models succeed or fail in cryosphere and monsoon Asia.

  • Snowmelt runoff diagnostics (HESS 2026)
  • Brahmaputra attribution (J. Hydrol. 2025)
🏗️
Human–River Systems

Anthropocene Hydrology

Floodplain urbanization maps, reservoir spectral analysis, and levee-effect reversals quantify how planning choices reshape risk.
