GeoWater Lab @ PKU

Research

We investigate the geography of global inland waters using hydrologic models, satellite observations, and data-driven earth system science.

Research Statement

I study the geography of global inland waters. I use hydrologic models at unprecedented spatial resolution, space observations, and data-driven methods to understand the fundamentals of surface water dynamics and its feedback to the climate system. My research takes a holistic earth system view to understand the global inland waters and its related fields.
