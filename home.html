About Me & Research

欢迎来到我的学术主页！我目前就职于北京大学，担任助理教授 [cite: 19, 21]。我们通过地球系统科学的方法研究全球水问题，重点关注全球尺度水文模型、遥感应用以及气候变化下的水循环研究 [cite: 42, 241]。

Our lab (GeoWater Lab @ PKU) studies the geography of global inland waters. We develop physically-based models, data-driven methods, and geographic information data infrastructures to improve our fundamental understanding of river-climate and river-human relationships [cite: 42, 43].
