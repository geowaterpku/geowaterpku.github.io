Core scientific questions

Three spatial questions guide our science.

Scroll through the questions that connect terrestrial water-cycle change, river-system organization, and hydrological prediction.

01 Terrestrial water dynamics

Climate × human influence

How do humans and climate change reshape terrestrial water dynamics?

We combine Earth-system models, satellite observations, and geospatial analysis to reveal how runoff, river flow, and floods are redistributed across landscapes and river networks.

Explore this research →

02 River-system organization

Pathways × geometry

How does the spatial organization of river systems regulate the movement of water?

We investigate how routing pathways, channel geometry, and floodplain structure govern the propagation, attenuation, storage, and energy dissipation of hydrological pulses.

Explore this research →

03 Spatially explicit prediction

Information × prediction

How can we develop spatially explicit approaches to advance hydrological prediction?

We develop spatial constraints, high-resolution routing, data–model fusion, and optimal parameterization strategies that make hydrological predictions more physically meaningful and locally informative.
