      • Kaihao presented his master thesis work on modeling flood regulation dynamics at the 2024 IGWG, and won the grand prize award.
      • Zimin presented his work on understanding river hydraulic geometry and won the first prize award in oral sessions.
      • Junwei presented his thesis work on spatio-temporal evolution of the Yangtze River Basin's flood regulation capacity and won the first prize poster award.
      • Haomei presented her work on river connectivity and won the second prize poster award.
      • Ziyun, Xiangyong, and Kaihao all traveled to major conferences across China.

      Dr. Lin receives the prestigious Boya Young Scientist Award and will be funded to work on human disturbances to global river flows in the next three years.
